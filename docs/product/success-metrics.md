# Success Metrics

## Primary

- Percentage of planning reviews reaching `APPLY_READY` without a second correction cycle.
- Blocking findings detected before `apply`.
- Applied implementation gaps detected before `verify`.
- Percentage of generated prompts accepted without added explanation.
- Average review cost by stage.
- Cost per change reaching `APPLY_READY`.
- Cost per change reaching `READY_FOR_VERIFY`.
- False-positive and escaped-defect rate from sampled human review.
- Time saved per review.

## Initial acceptance target

The first operational release must register at least two structurally different OpenSpec projects, complete all four review types, stay under the USD 10 monthly cap, persist all evidence metadata, and demonstrate that no excluded secret is sent.
